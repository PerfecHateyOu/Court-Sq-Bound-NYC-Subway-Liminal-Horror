LAST LOCAL: A Liminal Subway Horror Game (offline copy)

How to play:
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser. No internet, server or install needed.
3. Click "Board the Train", then click inside the train to look around with the mouse.

The game goes full screen when you board (turn this off in Settings). Press Esc, or use the
pause menu, to leave full screen.

Best in Chrome or Edge. Firefox and Safari should work too.
Sound starts after your first click (browsers require it).
Controls: mouse or arrow keys / WASD to look and walk, hold F to flag, 1/2/3 to choose,
C camera, R tape recorder, Tab evidence log, L route lines, Esc or P pause, M mute.
Your progress (Case Files, endings) is saved in the browser on this computer.
